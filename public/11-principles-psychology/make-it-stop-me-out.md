# Make it stop me out

> “Make it stop me out.”

The useful meaning is not passive stubbornness. It is precommitment. Before entry, define the evidence that invalidates the thesis, the protective order that enforces it, and the conditions that permit partial exits or a trailing stop.

Once filled, act only when one of those written conditions occurs. Do not take an unplanned early profit because open P&L feels fragile. Do not widen the stop because realizing a loss feels final. Both decisions replace tested market evidence with the emotional effect of the position.

## Operational rule

1. Write structural invalidation and expected stop fill.
2. Size the position so that loss is acceptable before entry.
3. Place and verify the protective order.
4. List every permitted management transition.
5. Exit or modify only when a listed condition is observed.
6. Treat any unlisted intervention as a process deviation, even if profitable.

{% hint style="warning" %}
The phrase never means ignoring new evidence. If a predefined early-exit or risk-control condition occurs, act immediately. Precommitment means following the complete plan, not waiting blindly for the original stop.
{% endhint %}

A stopped trade can be correctly executed. A profitable improvisation can be incorrectly executed. Review process and outcome separately.
