# Exhaustion

Exhaustion is a decline in aggressive participation as price reaches an extreme. Unlike absorption, where strong aggression is stopped by passive liquidity, exhaustion reflects insufficient initiative to continue the auction.

![Exhaustion shows declining aggressive effort at a price extreme](../.gitbook/assets/diagrams/effort-result.svg)

## Typical evidence

- Successive pushes cover less distance
- Volume and delta decline into the extreme
- Footprint prints show little aggressive participation at the final prices
- Price cannot attract follow-through outside the boundary
- Opposing flow then produces displacement

Low volume alone is ambiguous. It can mark exhaustion, but it can also mean thin liquidity that allows price to travel farther. The subsequent response distinguishes them.

## Context first

Test exhaustion after a mature directional auction reaches a preplanned target, higher-timeframe extreme, or poor high/low. Away from those locations, declining aggression has little reversal value.

| Feature | Absorption | Exhaustion |
|---|---|---|
| Aggressive effort | High | Declining |
| Why price stalls | Passive opposition | Initiative disappears |
| Confirmation | Defense holds and price displaces | Opposing initiative appears |

{% hint style="info" %}
Absorption and exhaustion can coexist: the last aggressive orders may be absorbed while participation fades.
{% endhint %}

**Study protocol:** Classify 100 session extremes as absorption, exhaustion, both, or neither using frozen charts. Record the first opposing displacement and measure classification precision against subsequent auction behavior.
